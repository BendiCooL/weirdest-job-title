Entry for the make.fun bounty [Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) — one job title, at most 60 characters, the weirdest one we can think of, judged blind head to head. Pot $5, free entry.

## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes one job title to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Professional Mourner for Strangers' Goldfish

44 characters. A professional mourner is a real job — hired grievers exist — and the twist is the client: a stranger's goldfish. Black suit, tiny coffin, a family that never met them.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The entry file holds exactly the title, with no trailing newline — the gate requires `/output/entry.txt` to be 1 to 60 characters. `main.ts` also refuses to write a title outside that range, or one that runs past a single line.

## Why this title

Against the judge brief: it *is* a job title, a few words naming an odd job; it is stranger than ordinary work, a real job with a gloriously odd twist; the joke lands on the first read; it sounds like a real job, specific enough to picture the person doing it; and at 44 characters it reads at a glance.
